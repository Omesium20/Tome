Anchor Bench

Tome · calibration ladders

Every rung, grouped by band

The ladder view checks one tag down its own scale. This view checks the scale across tags — the thing two independent passes cannot get right on their own. Read a band straight through and ask whether every card in it really belongs at that level. A 9–10 column with a merely-good card in it, or a 1–2 column holding something playable, is a rung that will drag every card scored against it.

This view follows your choices, not the candidate pool — it shows the card each rung is currently set to, so it reflects the ladder you are actually building.

A tag missing from this bench has no ladder on purpose. The vocabulary in schema.py is closed, and a member that cannot be graded from the card in front of the model — or that has no honest card at every band — is dropped rather than anchored. Notes at the foot of a ladder record any such call made while this pool was built.

Cards appearing in more than one ladder

Every ladder renders into one shared, cached system prompt, so a card that appears twice at two different ratings teaches the model the inconsistency the anchors exist to prevent. Same rating in two ladders is fine — it reinforces. Different ratings is a defect, and a difference that crosses a band boundary cannot be fixed by renumbering: the card has to be replaced in one of the two ladders.

Only chosen cards are checked here, not the whole candidate pool. Two candidates in different ladders may disagree harmlessly; it becomes a defect the moment you select both.

Your selection

Copy this into a file — say selection.json — and render the registry from it:

python -m knowledge_pipeline.anchor_bench export ranked.json --selection selection.json -o ladders.py

Only ladders you have marked reviewed appear — an unreviewed tag is left out rather than guessed, which is the distinction anchors.py exists to protect. The selection names the card each rung settled on rather than its position in the list, so it stays meaningful if the pool is re-ranked.
